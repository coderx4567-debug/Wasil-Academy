Learning the Quran with care and purpose.

At Wasil Academy, lessons move at the pace of the person reading, not a fixed curriculum clock. A teacher listens closely to how a student pronounces each letter, corrects gently, and repeats what needs repeating — because Quran reading is built through attention, not speed.

Every class is one-to-one, so a teacher can focus fully on where a student actually is: sounding out early letters, refining Tajweed rules, or working steadily through memorization and revision. Progress looks different for every learner, and lessons are shaped around that.

What an online lesson feels like

Each lesson takes place over a live video call between the student and their teacher. The teacher listens to recitation in real time, gives corrections as they happen, and keeps simple notes so both the family and the teacher can track what to revise next.

  • Live one-to-one video lesson
  • Real-time Tajweed correction
  • Simple lesson notes for revision
  • Ongoing progress awareness
